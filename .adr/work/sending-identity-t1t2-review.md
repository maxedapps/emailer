## Context-dependent concerns

- **Concern:** `EMAILER_SENDER_IDENTITY` is read by both stacks. Drift makes the From check pass on the Emailer's value while IAM covers only the referenced identity; send becomes IAM `AccessDenied` reported as `SubmissionUncertain`. Changing the owner-stack value plans a replacement and leaves a retained untracked identity.
- **Disposition:** Documented in T2 Risk/recovery, `.env.example:9-12`, and ADR-0009 Consequences. T4 asserts the two stacks agree. Not a T1/T2 defect.

- **Concern:** Passing a ref into `SendEmail` during Lambda construction is the partly documented path. Construction and plan-time resolution were researched; deploy, IAM grant, and a verifying send are T4.
- **Disposition:** T2 wired the path the plan specified. Live proof is T4, not missing T2 tests.

- **Concern:** `alchemy plan` does not print `removalPolicy`. Retention is confirmed by T3 `state read`.
- **Disposition:** Plan T1 Risk/recovery and T3 Verify. Retain is declared in source (`stacks/sending-identity.ts:25`).
