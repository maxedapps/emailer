## Verdict

**Revise.** Findings **1**, **2** and **3** must be addressed before implementation: T5 and the research section rest on an IAM fact that the code contradicts (`AudienceStoreLive` already binds `BatchGetItem` on both Lambdas); the store composition should call `allPrimitives` now that `CampaignStore` uses all six operations, with the two comments that would otherwise lie updated; and the merge protocol must name the three guaranteed conflicts with lane B instead of claiming they are avoided. Findings 4, 5 and 7 are small plan-text corrections worth making in the same pass; 8 to 11 trim tests that re-pin behaviour already pinned one layer down; 6 and 12 confirm the plan's two central technical claims. The mechanism itself — index attributes on the campaign item, ordering in `readEntityPage`, `campaignOf`, `page(Campaign, EntityCursor)`, `entityPageFlags` — is sound and is the right size.
