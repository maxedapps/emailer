## Verified (no finding)

**The dispatcher trace (the question that matters most).** With `if (filter !== undefined && !matchesFilter(filter, member.attributes)) { lastProcessed = member.id; continue; }` inserted at `Dispatching.ts:171` before `audience.addressStatus` (`:172`): a miss reaches none of `addressStatus` (`:172`), `skipRecipient` (`:176`), `consumeSlot` (`:193`), `claimRecipient` (`:208`) or `submitClaimed` (`:238`), so it costs no read, no transaction, no limiter slot and no submission; `lastProcessed` is set, so an overrun on a later member checkpoints past it (`:196-203`) and the continuation re-pages after it (`Membership.ts:224-227`, `ExclusiveStartKey`). The overrun path is strictly improved: `SliceOverrun` (`:197-198`) now fires only when the first *mailable* member's delay overruns, where before it also fired when a page's first member did. A page that is entirely filtered ends in `completeRun` or a checkpoint exactly as a page of already-claimed members does (`:253-259`). A redelivered or continued slice re-pages from the persisted cursor (`:141`), hydrates attributes afresh with a consistent batch read (`Membership.ts:245`, `contactOf(... stored.attributes ...)`; ADR-0005), and evaluates the same pure function: a member that matched on the first walk and stopped matching is skipped on the second and keeps its settled or unconfirmed row; one that started matching is claimed once under `attribute_not_exists(pk)` (`Storage/Campaigns.ts:437`). No path mails a matching member twice or skips one that matches at page time. "Attributes are read live at page time" is exactly what `listMembers` does today for membership (ADR-0011).
